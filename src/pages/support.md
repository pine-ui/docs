# Support Pine

Pine is free and open source. If it helps you build Unity interfaces, you can optionally support KbeniM's work on the library, documentation and examples.

[Buy me a coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/kbenim)

Contributions are handled on KbeniM's Buy Me a Coffee profile. You can also support Pine by reporting reproducible issues, sharing examples or contributing improvements through [GitHub](https://github.com/pine-ui/package).

Support is optional; it is not required to download or use Pine and does not purchase a support contract or additional license rights. This site does not collect payment details. Before paying, check the amount, whether the payment repeats, and the terms, fees and refund information shown by Buy Me a Coffee. Its own privacy policy and payment terms apply on that service.
